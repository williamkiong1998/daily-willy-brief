# The Willy Brief — source research

**Edition:** Friday, 28 August 2026 · Kuala Lumpur
**Research completed:** 28 August 2026 MYT

## Editorial spine

**Lead — Malaysia has more people in work; the next challenge is output.** DOSM's Q2 Labour Market Review reports employment, productivity per worker, and productivity per hour all increased year on year. The useful distinction is between a healthy labour market and a productivity system: lasting wage and business gains require tools, operating design, skills and investment that let each hour create more value.
