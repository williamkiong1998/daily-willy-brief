The Lead
Iran flatly denies Trump's “imminent” Hormuz deal, hours after another tanker was hit
Trump said Sunday a deal covering the Strait of Hormuz and Iran's denuclearisation was “imminent,” with direct US-Iran talks due to resume Monday afternoon. Iran's answer: “We currently have no negotiations with the United States,” foreign ministry spokesperson Esmail Baghaei told reporters Monday — Tehran says its only real talks are with Oman, over a new shipping corridor through Hormuz, and that no agreement has been reached. The denial lands days after a second LNG tanker, the GasLog Shanghai, was struck transiting the strait, and after Kuwait's air defences shot down Iranian drones aimed at a government facility over the weekend.

Why it matters: six months into this war, the two sides can't even agree on whether they're talking — which means whatever Washington calls progress this week, Tehran gets to call fiction.
The Takeaway
Sunday's “imminent” and Monday's “no such talks” aren't confused messaging — they're each side claiming exactly the amount of progress that plays best to its own audience. The negotiation that actually matters is the quiet one over the Oman shipping corridor; whether that hardens into something durable will tell you far more than either government's choice of adjective.

🤖 AI & Tech
xAI ships the fastest voice model yet, OpenAI opens its labs to 100,000 scientists for free, and China switches on a Nvidia-free gigawatt data centre
xAI's Grok Voice Think Fast 2.0 just became the fastest, most accurate voice model on the market: latency to first audio dropped from 1.25 seconds to 0.70, and it scored 82.9% on Artificial Analysis's speech-to-speech index versus GPT-Realtime-2.1's 79.1% and Gemini 3.1 Flash's 69.5%. It's $0.08 a minute and becomes the default Grok voice Wednesday.

OpenAI is giving 100,000 scientists free access to its frontier models, backed by a $250 million commitment through 2027. The first 10,000 researchers get GPT-5.6 Sol Pro, bigger context windows and 75+ life-science skills for genomics and drug discovery — their data excluded from training by default.

DeepSeek pushed V4-Flash into public beta at $0.14 per million input tokens, unchanged pricing but with a real capability bump. Independent benchmarking puts the cost per test at roughly 1/100th of a frontier US model — the pitch is still frontier capability at commodity pricing, even as rivals now cut prices to match it.

China's Z.AI switched on a 1-gigawatt AI data centre built entirely on domestic chips, no Nvidia hardware anywhere in the stack. As covered Friday with Meituan's chip-independent LongCat model, the independence bet has moved down a layer — the infrastructure no longer waits on a US export licence either.

Zenity raised $125 million, backed by SoftBank, Hitachi and LG, to secure AI agents wired into corporate systems. It's the second big “police the agents” round in a week after Onyx's $113 million (covered Monday) — the more autonomy enterprises hand agents, the more they'll pay someone to watch them.

A tourist plane crashed near Peru's Nazca Lines, killing all 13 aboard: the Aerodiana Cessna Caravan lost radio contact mid-flight Saturday, killing seven Italians, two Germans, two Spaniards and two crew. Aerodiana is grounded pending an investigation.

Spain confirmed at least 72 dead in the Ceuta border crisis, with local officials putting the toll as high as 86: some 60,000 migrants breached the enclave's frontier from Morocco in a single 48-hour stretch, many dying in a stampede at a breakwater barrier now getting a containment fence.

🔧 Product Craft
Stripe and Shopify just showed why your product page needs a second reader
Stripe co-developed the Agentic Commerce Protocol with OpenAI so a single structured product feed can power Instant Checkout inside ChatGPT — no bespoke integration, just a machine-readable schema for price, availability and fulfilment that any agent can parse. Shopify built the same idea into Agentic Storefronts: merchants describe their catalogue once in the admin, and it syndicates automatically to ChatGPT, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot at the same time.

The lesson for builders outside e-commerce: a growing share of demand now arrives pre-qualified by an agent that never rendered your landing page — it read a schema instead. If your product's source of truth still lives only in marketing copy and a pricing PDF, that traffic can't find you, no matter how good the copy is.
