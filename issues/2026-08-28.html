The Willy Brief
Friday, 28 August 2026 · Kuala Lumpur
Malaysia’s labour market is healthy. The more interesting question is whether the systems around its people—tools, capital, skills and operating design—can turn that health into more valuable output per hour. Today’s brief follows that question from a national productivity release to the way an AI product should measure completed work.
The Lead
Malaysia’s next growth test is not jobs. It is output.
DOSM’s Q2 review shows a labour market with breadth and momentum: employment reached 16.77 million, while unemployment held at 3.0%. The more useful number sits one level below the headline. Value added per employed person rose 4.9% year on year to RM26,595; per hour worked it rose 5.5% to RM46.50. Those are encouraging outcomes, not an automatic productivity dividend. A country can add workers and still leave value on the table if technology, management practices, training, finance and market access do not let teams do higher-value work.

Why it matters: GDP growth tells you the economy is moving. Productivity tells you whether its operating system is improving. For a builder, that distinction is personal: a new feature is activity; a faster, more reliable customer outcome is output.
The Takeaway
Employment is an input; productivity is the conversion rate. Malaysia’s next edge will come from making good work repeatable through better tools, decision rights and access to customers—not merely adding more activity to the day.
🤖 AI & Tech
The model matters—but so do the work and rules around it
01
A randomised 1,000+-student experiment found ChatGPT and critical-thinking training do different jobs. ChatGPT access improved rubric scores by almost one point on a five-point scale; causal-reasoning training yielded more distinct ideas. The result is not “AI makes students smarter.” It is a design clue: measure polished output and originality separately, or optimise only for the answer that looks best.
02
Salesforce and Anthropic say Claudeforce will pair Claude with 37 prebuilt sales skills. The announced pilot routes actions back through Salesforce, where business rules apply; open beta is expected in September. That is the important architecture: model reasoning proposes a move, while the system that owns the record enforces permission, process and auditability.
03
OpenAI is opening commercial operations in Brazil, one of its three largest weekly-active-user markets. The company reports roughly 215 million daily ChatGPT messages there and says 35% of classified individual-account messages are work-related. Treat those as company-reported indicators, but the pattern travels: local adoption becomes durable when training, language, community and business workflows arrive with the model.

🧠 Builder’s Notebook
Measurement contracts: define what an agent actually completed
Intuition: a measurement contract is the receipt that proves work happened at the right standard. Asking an agent to “handle support” is like paying a restaurant for “food activity.” Better: define a resolved case, the quality threshold, the human override and the reversal path. Salesforce’s self-defined Agentic Work Units are a useful prompt—not a universal proof of value.

Mechanism:
user task → completed-work unit + quality condition → event log → business outcome → product and pricing decision
A count only becomes meaningful when the boundary is explicit. Did the agent draft a reply, send it, solve the customer’s problem, or create a costly correction? Instrument the state transition and the exception, then link it to the outcome that matters: time saved, conversion, retention, cost or trust.

Try this (20 min): choose one AI flow. Write one completed-work unit, its quality test, a human-override event and a reversal event. Then decide which business outcome the unit is allowed to influence. You now have a measurement contract rather than a vanity counter.
