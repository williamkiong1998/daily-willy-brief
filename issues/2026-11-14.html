☕ Cold Open
Agents can now move closer to a transaction, not merely recommend one. OpenAI's Wallet announcement makes the real product question painfully concrete: where does the agent stop, and where does the person take responsibility?
The Lead

The checkout button gets an agent

OpenAI says ChatGPT Wallet lets agents in ChatGPT Work, Codex and dots complete purchases with user approval and existing card protections. The announcement is narrow, and the available first-party feed does not establish merchants, regions, payment rails or transaction limits. Still, the product boundary is clear enough to matter. Search and recommendation leave the user with the last mile. A wallet creates a stateful handoff between intent and money. That handoff needs an unmistakable approval moment, a record of what was purchased, and a sensible reversal path. The interesting race is not who can make an agent browse most freely. It is who makes delegated spending feel legible when the answer turns out to be wrong. Why it matters: once an agent can initiate a purchase, approval design becomes core product infrastructure.
THE TAKEAWAY
An approval click is not a legal flourish at the end of an agent flow. It is the point where a product must expose the exact merchant, price, scope and consequence, because that is where a person can still change their mind.

🧠 Builder's Notebook
Approval state is a product object
Intuition: an approval is not the same as a modal with a green button. Think of it as a signed purchase order. It has a requester, a fixed scope, an expiry, an outcome and a history. If any of those can change after the person sees the request, the system is asking for trust without offering a real decision.

Mechanism:
agent intent → immutable price and scope → named human approval → bounded payment → receipt and reversal path
Application: Wallet-style flows make this concrete. Do not let an agent's approved authority float across merchants, price changes or a later session. Bind the confirmation to the exact action, expire it quickly and save the receipt with the agent trace. Try this (20 min): take one agent action that would spend money or change a customer record. Draft the approval payload with six fields: target, amount or scope, rationale, expiry, approver and undo path. Then ask which one the current interface cannot show. That blank is the design work.
